Order & Customer Data Synchronization

Rishvi CRM enables structured and automated synchronization of customers, orders, shipping data, and pricing between Odoo CRM and Linnworks.

It ensures that customer records, sales orders, and order statuses remain consistent across both systems, providing complete visibility and control over the order lifecycle.

Customer Data Sync

Synchronize customer details from Linnworks into Odoo CRM. Customer records are automatically created or updated with correct billing and shipping information, ensuring accurate order association and reporting.

Bulk Order Synchronization

Import large volumes of orders from Linnworks using controlled batch processing. Supports page-based imports, allowing safe and efficient synchronization of hundreds of orders while maintaining system stability.

Order-Level Updates

Manage individual orders by importing or refreshing specific Linnworks order numbers. Ideal for troubleshooting, reprocessing failed orders, or validating customer-specific transactions without running a full sync.

Reliable Sync & Validation

Built-in validation logic ensures accurate order mapping, prevents duplicate records, and maintains data integrity across CRM and Sales modules in Odoo.

Order Status Visibility

Provides real-time visibility of order statuses across both systems. Users can easily track whether orders are pending, processed, dispatched, cancelled, or returned-ensuring transparency throughout the sales process.

CRM & Order Management Features

Rishvi CRM is designed to streamline customer management, sales orders, and Linnworks synchronization while ensuring accurate data flow across systems.

It simplifies daily order operations and provides full visibility into customers, sales, inventory, and order statuses within Odoo.

Customer Management - Create, sync, and manage customers from Linnworks in Odoo CRM with accurate billing and shipping details.
Order Import & Sync - Import single or bulk orders from Linnworks into Odoo Sales with automatic customer and product mapping.
Sales Order Processing - Convert imported orders into sales orders, confirm them, and sync back to Linnworks with complete order details.
Shipping & Country Mapping - Automatically sync shipping services and countries to ensure correct order routing and address validation.
Order Status Tracking - Track order lifecycle across both systems including pending, processed, dispatched, cancelled, and returned orders.
Bulk Order Operations - Process large volumes of orders using page-based imports for safe and efficient synchronization.
Data Validation & Error Handling - Built-in validation prevents duplicate orders, mismatched data, and sync failures across CRM and Sales modules.
Inventory & Pricing Consistency - Ensure inventory levels and pricing remain aligned during order synchronization between Odoo and Linnworks.
Audit Logs & Sync Flags - Track sync status using flags and logs for better monitoring, troubleshooting, and reporting.
Customer-Order Linking - Automatically link customers with their sales orders, order history, and Linnworks references for complete customer visibility inside Odoo CRM.
