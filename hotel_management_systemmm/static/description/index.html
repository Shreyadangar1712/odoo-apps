Hotel Management Features

The Hotel Management system is designed to streamline reservations, guest services, and hotel operations while ensuring smooth coordination between departments.

It simplifies hotel workflows and provides full visibility into guest stays, services used, and billing operations.

Reservation Management - Create and manage guest reservations with real-time room availability and booking scheduling.
Guest Check-In Process - Quickly check guests into their reserved rooms while recording guest details and assigning room access.
Room Allocation Management - Assign available rooms to guests and manage occupancy status including available, occupied, and maintenance rooms.
Service Request Management - Guests can request additional services such as housekeeping, laundry, or maintenance which can be tracked and assigned to staff.
Guest Stay Tracking - Track active guest stays including check-in time, services requested, and expected checkout date.
Food & Room Service Orders - Guests can order food directly from their room, and the system records all food and beverage orders under the guest account.
Vehicle & Transportation Services - Manage guest transportation requests such as airport pickups, city travel, and vehicle bookings.
Event & Meeting Management - Guests or organizations can book event spaces, conference rooms, or banquet halls with proper scheduling and resource management.
Service Tracking & Task Management - Track service requests assigned to housekeeping, kitchen, or maintenance teams.
Guest Billing & Checkout Management - All services used during the stay are automatically added to the guest bill and processed during checkout.
