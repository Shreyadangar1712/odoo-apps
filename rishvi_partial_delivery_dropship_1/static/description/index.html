Overview

The Partial Delivery Dropshipping module automatically handles the fulfillment of sales orders by delivering available stock quantities and creating dropshipping purchase orders for any excess quantities. Designed for businesses that need to manage partial deliveries efficiently, it offers the following features:

  • Delivers the available product quantity (up to 200 units or less).
  • Creates dropshipping purchase orders for any excess quantity beyond available stock.
  • Seamlessly integrates with Sales, Inventory, and Purchase modules for a streamlined process.

Ideal for businesses aiming to optimize their sales and inventory management processes while ensuring quick fulfillment and dropshipping integration.
