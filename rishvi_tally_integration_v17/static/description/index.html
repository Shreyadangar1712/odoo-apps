Real Estate Management Features

The Real Estate Management system is designed to streamline property operations, reservations, sales, and rental workflows while ensuring smooth coordination between teams and departments.

It simplifies real estate processes and provides full visibility into property status, transactions, and financial activities.

Property Management - Create and manage properties, buildings, and units with complete structural details including floors, unit types, and locations.
Reservation Handling - Manage property bookings with real-time availability and automated reservation workflows.
Unit Allocation Management - Assign units to customers and track status such as available, reserved, sold, or leased.
Deposit & Payment Tracking - Capture reservation deposits and link them directly with customer bookings and accounting records.
Customer Interaction Tracking - Track all customer interactions including inquiries, bookings, and transaction history.
Ownership Contract Management - Convert reservations into ownership contracts with structured pricing and installment plans.
Rental Contract Management - Create and manage rental agreements with recurring billing and contract duration tracking.
Installment & Invoice Management - Generate invoices for property sales or rentals and track payments against each transaction.
Financial Integration - All payments, invoices, and refunds are automatically linked with accounting for accurate reporting.
Reporting & Analytics - Access detailed reports on sales, rentals, payments, and property performance.
