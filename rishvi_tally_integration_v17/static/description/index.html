Accounting is one of the most important subjects for any business; as not managing accounting properly can lead to huge loss to the businesses. Accounting facilitates you to keep a check on the inward as well as outward transactions of a business. It is a systematic process to calculate the transactions of the company to measure the monetary terms as well as record into the different accounts.

Perks of managing Accounting flawlessly:

You can track and analyze the performance of your business
It helps you to ensure that liabilities of your business are appropriately looked
You can create a budget and project plan for upcoming years
You can also file final statement of your business

Tally is one of the most popular accounting software in India. Basically, it is used for financial accounting purposes like daily accounting, balance sheet finalization, inventory maintenance, generating reports for tax filing, etc.

When talking about Business management; Odoo is one of the best software to manage all business operations successfully. Isn't it great when the leading business management solution is amalgamated with the best Accounting solution!

Connecting your Tally account with Odoo!

Odoo Tally Connector is a flawless solution to perfectly connect the two amazing solutions that are Odoo as well as Tally. The module allows you to import and export all the important data to manage the accounting.

Export Accounting data in XLS Format

Odoo Tally Connector allows you to easily export the data in XLS format so that it can be easily used for tax filing.

Benefits of Tally Accounting than Manual Process

You just need to make a Ledger Name, and then pass entries. Ledgers, Trial Balance, and Balance Sheet are updated automatically in Tally.
There are less chances of error and less time consuming. You just need to make sure that the accounting entry should be correct; otherwise, it increases the chance of errors.
Moreover, the Balance Sheet is tallied with each entity automatically.
