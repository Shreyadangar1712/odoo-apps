Jewellery Management Features

The Jewellery Management System provides a comprehensive set of features to simplify and streamline jewellery business operations including inventory, sales, and customer management.

Product Selection: Manage detailed jewellery products with attributes like weight, purity, and design variations
Payment Processing: Handle multiple payment methods and ensure accurate billing for every transaction
Discount Application: Apply making charges, wastage, and discounts dynamically during sales
Live Inventory Sync: Track stock levels in real-time across all jewellery items and categories
Receipt Generation: Generate accurate invoices and receipts with complete product and pricing details
Offline Mode: Continue operations smoothly with data handling even in low connectivity scenarios
Barcode Scanning: Use barcode support for quick product identification and faster billing
User Access Control: Manage roles and permissions to control access across different users
Accounting Integration: Integrate with accounting modules for seamless financial tracking and reporting
Session End Summary: Generate daily sales summaries and reports for better business insights
